How it works
Three steps.
That's it.
01
Paste your URL
Drop any URL into the field above — http or https. Optionally set an expiry, a click limit, or a password.
02
We compress it
A 6-character Base62 slug is generated and stored. Google Safe Browsing scans the destination in the background. You get the short link instantly.
03
Share it
Copy the link, download a QR code, or open the manage page. Your token is stored in this browser — use it to edit or delete later.
04
Watch it work
Every click is buffered in Redis and flushed to the database every 10 seconds. Your manage page shows live click counts in real time.
